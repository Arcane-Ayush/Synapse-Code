# Welcome Everyone! 🚀

We are thrilled to announce the launch of the **official Google Club website**! This platform will serve as the central hub for all our activities, projects, and resources.

## What's New?

1.  **Dynamic Activity Calendar**: Never miss an event again. Check out the [Activities](/activities) page to see what's coming up.
2.  **Project Showcase**: Explore the amazing work done by our members in the [Projects](/projects) section.
3.  **Team Directory**: Get to know the people behind the club on our [Team](/team) page.
4.  **Blog**: You're reading it right now! We'll be posting tutorials, recaps, and announcements here.

## Why a Website?

We wanted a space that truly reflects our community's creativity and technical skills. This site isn't just a static page; it's a playground for us to experiment with new technologies like **React**, **Three.js**, and **Framer Motion**.

## Get Involved

Want to contribute to the site? The source code is open source! Join our Discord to learn how you can help build the next feature.

Stay tuned for more updates!
